Standard RUET Lab Report Submission Rules

Follow these instructions carefully to avoid mark deductions.

1. Front Page & Margin

Use official RUET ME cover layout. Ensure 1-inch margins on all sides with offset left for binding.

2. Data & Calculations

Include raw data tables signed by the course teacher during the experiment period.

3. Discussions & Conclusion

Discussion section must be strictly handwritten with individual observations and error analysis.
